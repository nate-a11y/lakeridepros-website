1. Sign in and know your role

Coordinator access is approval-only. Sign in with the approved mobile number connected to your coordinator profile and the six-digit code sent by text.

The same secure sign-in page routes approved users to the correct participant or coordinator view.

  1. 1Enter your approved mobile number.

    Use the number assigned to your coordinator access.

  2. 2Select “Text me a code.”

    Enter the six-digit one-time code from the SMS.

  3. 3Use the coordinator navigation.

    Coordinator, Participant snapshots, and Request for a rider are your primary work areas.

  4. 4Sign out when finished.

    This is especially important on any shared phone, tablet, or computer.

Role boundary: Coordinator access includes confidential participant, ride, cost, and reporting information. It does not grant access to unrelated Lake Ride Pros operations or customer records.

3. Review a request and move it forward

Open the request to verify the participant, date, timing, route, notes, current status, and any prior conversation before taking action.

Example screen. Available controls change with the request’s current status and active action.

Pending

Review the request. A coordinator may acknowledge it, request more information, or decline it.

Acknowledged

The request has been reviewed and processing/scheduling is underway.

Needs information

Add a clear participant-visible question. A participant reply becomes Information received for review.

Confirmed

An actual Lake Ride Pros trip has been scheduled and linked to the request.

Acknowledged is not Confirmed. Never tell a participant the ride is confirmed solely because the request was reviewed. Confirmation occurs only after Lake Ride Pros schedules and links the actual trip.

4. Handle changes, cancellations, and messages

Participant changes and cancellations stay attached to the original ride reference. The original ride remains visible while the action is processed.

Acknowledge an action when review begins; complete it only after the operational change is finished.

✓

Acknowledge

Tells the participant the action was reviewed and is now being processed.

●

Complete

Use after the requested change is finished. Completing a cancellation moves the ride to Cancelled.

×

Decline

Restores the prior ride status. Add a clear participant-visible explanation.

Conversation rules: Messages are shared with the participant, text-only, and immutable. They cannot be edited or deleted. Keep sensitive internal commentary out of the participant conversation.

5. Submit a request for a participant

Use Request for a rider when an approved participant needs help submitting a transportation request.

Choose the approved participant first so the correct pickup locations become available.

  1. 1Select the approved participant.

    The portal records that the coordinator submitted on the participant’s behalf.

  2. 2Complete the ride details.

    Ride type, date, pickup time, required-arrival time, and one-way/round-trip direction are required.

  3. 3Use approved locations.

    Select the participant’s approved pickup and a managed destination.

  4. 4Submit and track it.

    The request begins Pending and follows the same review and confirmation process.

Location not listed? Submit it for Lake Ride Pros approval. The location cannot be used until approved, and submitting the location does not guarantee transportation there.

6. Use participant snapshots and reporting

Participant snapshots combine the approved profile, Treatment Court information, ride accountability, approved locations, program costs, and the final separate-personal-transportation indicator.

Example screen uses sample participant and cost information.

Participant snapshots

  • Filter program-to-date, current month, previous month, or custom dates
  • Review phase, scheduled/completed/cancelled rides, no-shows, and cancellation rate
  • Review coordinator-only cost and approved profile/location details

Program reporting

  • Export the currently filtered request queue to CSV
  • Use month-to-date and contract-to-date spending from trip records
  • Review monthly invoice records when issued; payments are not processed here

Data timing: Ride activity and costs come from Lake Ride Pros trip records and may take up to 15 minutes to appear after an operational update.

Coordinator Quick Reference

Make every handoff clear

The coordinator portal manages requests and accountability. Lake Ride Pros remains the source of truth for the scheduled trip and its operational status.

Review daily

  • Needs attention and information received
  • Pending requests approaching the review target
  • Active changes and cancellations
  • Acknowledged requests waiting for scheduling

Communicate clearly

  • Use participant-visible explanations when required
  • Ask one clear question when information is missing
  • Keep the shared conversation operational and necessary
  • Do not promise confirmation before the linked trip exists

Protect participant data

  • Use coordinator information only for authorized program work
  • Do not share screenshots, CSV exports, or participant details
  • Keep unnecessary medical information out of notes/messages
  • Sign out on shared devices

Escalate correctly

  • Portal conversations are for non-urgent request communication
  • Use the configured urgent-support path for immediate ride help
  • Portal messages are not monitored for emergencies
  • Emergency or immediate danger: call 911
