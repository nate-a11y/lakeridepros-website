1. Sign in securely

No password is needed. Portal access works only for mobile numbers approved for the Camden County transportation program.

Example screen. Enter the same mobile number that was approved for your participant profile.

  1. 1Enter your approved mobile number.

    Use the number connected to your Treatment Court transportation access.

  2. 2Select “Text me a code.”

    A six-digit, one-time sign-in code will arrive by SMS.

  3. 3Enter the six-digit code.

    If it expires or never arrives, complete the security check and request a new one.

  4. 4Accept the transportation rules.

    Before your first request, review and accept the current notice, location, change, and cancellation rules.

Can’t sign in? Recheck the phone number first. Unknown or unapproved numbers cannot create their own account. Contact your Treatment Court coordinator or Lake Ride Pros for help.

2. Submit a transportation request

From My rides, select Submit a new request — or choose New request in the menu.

Example screen. Your actual ride types and approved locations may be different.

Enter the ride details

  • Ride type and ride date
  • Requested pickup time and appointment / required-arrival time
  • One way or round trip
  • For round trips: a return time or Call when ready

Choose approved locations

  • Select an approved pickup and destination
  • Add companion count/details when shown
  • Add short operational notes only
  • Select Submit request and watch for the success message

Important: Submitting a request does not guarantee transportation. The ride is confirmed only after Lake Ride Pros completes scheduling and the portal shows Confirmed.

3. Get the request right the first time

A complete request helps the team review and schedule your transportation faster.

Advance notice

Read the notice shown after choosing a ride type. Some appointments may require advance notice. Same-day drug-test requests may be allowed, but they are not guaranteed.

Pickup vs. arrival

Enter both when you want to be picked up and when you must arrive. Lake Ride Pros may adjust pickup timing while scheduling.

Location not listed?

Select Ask for another approved location. The location cannot be selected for a ride until it has been reviewed and approved.

Possible duplicate?

If another request exists for a similar date and time, the portal will warn you. Submit another only if it is truly a separate ride.

Before you select “Submit request”

Do include

  • The correct appointment date and arrival time
  • Whether you need one-way or round-trip service
  • A companion count, if applicable
  • Useful pickup instructions, such as an entrance

Do not include

  • Diagnoses or unnecessary medical information
  • An unapproved address in the notes field
  • Multiple ride dates in one request
  • An emergency or immediate dispatch request

One ride date = one request

Need the same ride again? Open the prior ride and use Duplicate request, then choose a new date and time.

Late / urgent requests: The portal may still allow a request after the normal cutoff, but it will be marked late / urgent and fulfillment is not guaranteed.

4. Track the status on “My rides”

You will receive text alerts for important updates. Sign back in to see the full status, request details, and any message from the team.

Example screen. Requests in progress and confirmed rides are shown separately.

Pending

Submitted and waiting for review.

Acknowledged

Reviewed; processing or scheduling is underway. This is not yet confirmation.

Needs information

The team needs an answer. Open the request and reply in the conversation.

Confirmed

A Lake Ride Pros trip is scheduled and linked. Review the trip details.

Change requested

Your requested change is attached to the ride and being reviewed.

Cancelled / Declined / No show

A final or exception outcome. Open the ride to review any explanation.

Ride accountability: The dashboard also shows your Treatment Court phase and ride counts for the selected period. Synced ride activity may take up to 15 minutes to appear.

Quick Reference

Know what to do next

The portal is the source for your current request status and confirmed trip details.

Before requesting

  • Use your approved mobile number to sign in.
  • Read the ride type’s notice requirement.
  • Have the appointment date and arrival time ready.
  • Choose only approved pickup and destination locations.

After requesting

  • Look for the “Request submitted” message.
  • Watch for text alerts and sign back in for details.
  • Remember: Acknowledged is not Confirmed.
  • Open the request if the team needs more information.

If plans change

  • Edit notes directly only while the request is Pending.
  • After review, use Request a change or Request cancellation.
  • Wait for the action to be completed.
  • For a repeated ride, use Duplicate request.

If you need help

  • Non-urgent request question: use the request conversation.
  • Immediate ride help: use Call urgent support inside the portal.
  • Emergency or immediate danger: call 911.
  • General Lake Ride Pros help: (573) 206-9499.
